Lessons

I teach voice lessons in and around Ballard for singers at any stage. I have vocal pedagogy training from St. Olaf College, and I sing opera, choral pieces, and art song with many groups around Seattle.

I also teach beginning piano. Piano students will need their own piano to practice on at home.

Location

In-home lessons

I can bring a keyboard to your home or rehearsal space, so you do not need a piano one for voice lessons.

Lesson space in Ballard

A dedicated lesson space in Ballard is to come. Email me for the current details.

Lessons are tailored to each student and their goals. I will work with you to set those goals and choose music. Lessons will include warm-ups, bel canto technical guidance, and repertoire work.

Rates

Lessons are $40 per half hour and $60 per hour.
