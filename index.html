Gabriel Salmon

About

Gabe sings bass-baritone in opera, choral, and art song, both as a soloist and with ensembles around Seattle. Those include Rising Waters Collective, NOISE, Harmonia Orchestra & Chorus, The Swedish Singers, and Blessed Sacrament Parish. Before moving to Seattle, he sang with Minnesota Opera, Mixed Precipitation's Picnic Operetta, and the Gilbert & Sullivan Light Opera Company.

Gabe teaches voice and beginning piano lessons in Ballard. See Lessons for details.
